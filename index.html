🔴 Задание 4: Уровень Causative + Vocabulary (Интеграция грамматики)

Объедините два предложения в одно, используя каузативную структуру (have/get something done) И слово в скобках.

Пример:
Prompt: Someone decorated my office. They did a terrible job. (workmanship)
Ответ: I had my office decorated, but the workmanship is terrible.

1. Someone installed a new security system. They broke my door. (reasonable care and skill)

2. Someone cleaned my carpets. They only did half the job. (partial refund)

3. Someone repaired my phone. The screen fell off the next day. (to re-do)

4. Someone painted my house. They hired another company to do it, and that company ruined the walls. (third-party)

5. Someone built a new fence. They stopped working after two days. (breach of contract)

6. Someone tailored my dress. They made it the wrong size. (exercise my right)

7. Someone fixed my boiler. They left a huge mess. (workmanship)

Примерные ответы:
1. I had my security system installed, but they showed a lack of reasonable care and skill.
2. I got my carpets cleaned, but I want a partial refund because they only did half the job.
3. I had my phone repaired, but now I expect them to re-do the work.
4. I had my house painted, but the main company used a third-party who ruined the walls.
5. I am having a new fence built, but stopping after two days is a breach of contract.
6. I had my dress tailored, but I will exercise my right to get a refund.
7. I had my boiler fixed, but the workmanship was terrible.

🟣 Задание 5: Уровень Error Correction (Найди и исправь ошибку)

В каждом предложении есть одна ошибка (грамматическая или лексическая). Найдите и исправьте предложения.

1. I need to have my car re-do because the mechanic did a terrible workmanship.

2. I got my hair cutted, but they didn't show reasonable care and skill.

3. The shop broke our agreement, so I am going to do my right to exercise a partial refund.

4. You need to have your roof checked by a third-party if you want to make a partial refund.

5. I had my floor installed by a professional, but now I want them to re-did it because of the poor workmanship.

Правильные ответы:
1. re-done (have my car re-done) / the workmanship was terrible (или: did a terrible job).
2. cut (got my hair cut).
3. exercise my right to ask for a partial refund.
4. You make a claim / You don't "make" a refund, you "request/demand" one (лучше: You need to ask for a partial refund).
5. re-do (want them to re-do it).

🔵 Задание 6: Уровень Production (Говорение)

Ответьте на вопросы вслух. Постарайтесь использовать лексику и каузативную структуру.

  1. Think about a time you had a service done (e.g., repairs, cleaning). Was the workmanship good or bad? What did you do?
  2. If a company tells you to contact a third-party to fix a problem they caused, how would you respond? What would you say about reasonable care and skill?
  3. You paid for a 3-hour professional service, but the workers left after 1 hour. What do you demand? (Use breach of contract and partial refund).
  4. Have you ever had to get a service re-done by someone else because the first professional lacked reasonable care and skill? Describe it.
  5. Imagine you had your house painted, but they spilled paint. How do you exercise your right to get compensation?
  6. A company refuses to give you a partial refund for a bad service. What legal steps would you take next?
  7. Why is it important to check the workmanship immediately after you have something done?
